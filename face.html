Latin Women Empowered: Faces

Celebrating leadership, identity, and empowerment.

About the Event

This event highlighted the stories and journeys of Latina women making an impact across industries, media, and community leadership.

Through powerful conversations and shared experiences, attendees explored identity, resilience, and the importance of representation.

The evening created a space for connection, inspiration, and continued growth among women shaping the future.
